About

About 371 wordsAbout 1 min

AI engineer, currently pursuing a research Master's (MRes) in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems.

My past work centred on R&D and production delivery in computer vision, OCR, document intelligence, and LLM applications — building systems for document structuring, OCR + LLM, RAG / GraphRAG, Agent Workflow, and model inference deployment.

My research interest now focuses on AI4Math, LLM Reasoning, and Geometry Problem Solving — how models can combine visual information, structured representations, and symbolic tools to reason more reliably over many steps.

I write here about papers I read, things I learn, and engineering practice.
